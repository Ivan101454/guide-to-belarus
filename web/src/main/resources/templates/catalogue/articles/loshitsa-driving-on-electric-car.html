Driving a electric car in Loshitsa park

Last Saturday at midday, we went to Loshitsa Park, the biggest and most beautiful park in Minsk.

We wanted to rent bicycles, but there weren’t any that we liked.

Instead, we found a place that rented kids’ vehicles, and it had adult models too. They were like small electric cars—fast and fun to drive.

We rented the vehicle for one hour. After the couple before us returned it, we got in and started our ride.
We drove on all the park paths, and we really enjoyed it.

At home, I made sandwiches with cheese, pepperoni, and cucumber. We sat on a blanket in the grass and had lunch with the sandwiches and some cola.
