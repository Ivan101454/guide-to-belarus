The Zoo That Felt Like a Dream

Part One

We saw a big board with a map of the Zoo.

The zoo has two parts.
The first part was where we were at that moment. It names a Fairy World.

It had many decorations like country houses and fences and sculptures of characters from fairy tales. All were made from wood.

I saw two powerfully bulls with huge horns. They were strong, but calm.
I didn’t expect them to look so close. Only a moat separated us.

We saw a tall hairy donkey. He ate grass. I was surprised how big a donkey might be

Standing in front of us were Llamas. They were beautiful with brown color on the back.
The llama stood by the fence and was asking for food.

I couldn’t believe how arrogant camels were. They looked at us so haughty. The camel had two humps.

One of my favourites was when we went to the fairy castle. It was surrounded by a water canal.
There were many fairy-tale characters from wood. In the canal were ducks and a swan.

We went into the castle, and inside it felt like a spooky, dark, mystic place.
There were bats, small monkeys, and other tiny animals living inside.
The decorations reminded me of an Indiana Jones movie - old statues, jungle vines and jungle animals.

By the castle wall, there were many birds. I like birds and watched them with great joy.
I also saw a great wild cat. It was a very graceful animal.

Our visit to the first part of the zoo had ended.
We sat in the car and drove to the second part of the zoo.
